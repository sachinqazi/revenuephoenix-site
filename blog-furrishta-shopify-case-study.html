Building the Furrishta Shopify Storefront

A custom Liquid-built Shopify D2C website, plus packaging and inventory systems, for an Indian pet accessories brand.

Home › Blog › Building the Furrishta Shopify Storefront

Furrishta came to us as an Indian pet accessories brand needing a Shopify D2C website built from the ground up - not just a storefront, but packaging direction and inventory systems working together with it.

A Custom Liquid Build

We built Furrishta's storefront using custom Shopify Liquid development rather than a stock theme - giving the brand a storefront that matches its actual product range and merchandising needs instead of being boxed in by a template's limitations.

Packaging as Part of the Brand, Not an Afterthought

For a pet accessories brand, unboxing is part of the product experience. We worked on packaging design alongside the storefront so the brand feels consistent from the product page to the doorstep.

Inventory Systems That Actually Hold Up

A growing catalog needs inventory that doesn't fall apart at scale. We set up inventory systems built around Furrishta's actual SKU count and restocking patterns, not a one-size-fits-all setup.
