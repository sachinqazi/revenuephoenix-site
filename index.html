Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Shopify store cost with Revenue Phoenix?

Shopify development packages start at $800, brand strategy starts at $500, and combo packages bundle both for stores that need design, development, and positioning together. See our Services section for current pricing.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes - we currently serve clients across the USA and India, combining US market UX expectations with a founder-led team based in India.

Is the deposit refundable?

No - our 50% upfront deposit is non-refundable. It secures your project slot and covers initial discovery and planning work. Full details are in our Refund Policy.

What happens after my website launches?

You get post-launch support and no lock-in contracts - we stay reachable for questions and fixes after handover, not just during the build.

Do you offer revisions if I'm not happy with the work?

Yes. If something isn't right within the agreed scope, we prioritize revisions first - refunds are a last resort, not a substitute for the revision process. See our Refund Policy for how this works.

Are you based in Agra?

Yes - Revenue Phoenix is a founder-led marketing agency and website development company based in Agra, India, working with clients locally and across India, the USA, Canada, the UAE, and Australia.

Are you a full digital marketing company, or just web development?

Both. Revenue Phoenix is a digital marketing company covering Shopify/website development, social media management, and Meta Ads - brands can hire us for one service or all three together.

Do you build Shopify D2C websites?

Yes - Shopify D2C website development is one of our core services, from custom Liquid builds to Dawn theme rebuilds. See our portfolio for recent work.

Do you offer social media management services?

Yes - social media management is available standalone or bundled with website development, covering content planning, consistent posting, and monthly reporting.
