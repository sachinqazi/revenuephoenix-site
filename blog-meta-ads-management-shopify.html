What Meta Ads Management Should Look Like for a Shopify Store

Search 'Meta ads management agency' and most results look identical. Here's what should actually separate a real partner.

Home › Blog › What Meta Ads Management Should Look Like for a Shopify Store

"Meta ads management agency" is a crowded search term, and most results look identical. Here's what should actually separate a real Meta ads partner from someone just boosting posts.

Facebook Ads for a Shopify Store Aren't Generic Ads

Campaign structure should be built around your actual catalog and Shopify's own pixel and conversion tracking - not a generic "boost this post" approach that can't tell you which ad actually drove a sale.

What a Real Paid Social Media Agency Tests

Creative variants, audience segments, and placements - systematically, not just running one ad and hoping. Testing is what turns ad spend into a repeatable channel instead of a gamble.

Questions to Ask Before You Hire

"What's your reporting cadence?" Monthly is too slow to catch a failing campaign early.

"Do you optimize for the platform's algorithm or fight it?" Meta's own delivery system works best with a certain volume of data - an agency that understands this spends your budget more efficiently.
