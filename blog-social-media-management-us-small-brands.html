Social Media Management Built for Small US Brands

Most social media pricing pages are built for enterprise budgets. Here's what actually matters for a small US brand deciding what to pay for.

Home › Blog › Social Media Management Built for Small US Brands

Most social media handling cost breakdowns and social media management pricing pages are built for enterprise budgets. Here's what actually matters for a small US brand deciding what to pay for.

What "Basic" Should Actually Include

Consistent posting on your core platform, a real content calendar, and design that matches your brand - not templated graphics.

What Separates a Real Strategy From a Posting Calendar

A posting calendar keeps you visible. A strategy ties content to a goal - email list growth, product launches, or direct sales - and reports on whether it's working.

The Questions That Reveal the Truth

"What's the plan if a post underperforms?" A real manager adjusts. A poster just keeps posting.

"Can I see actual results, not just content samples?" Engagement growth, follower quality, or traffic driven - ask for a number.

What This Should Cost

Pricing scales with platform count, posting frequency, and whether paid social is included. Get specifics before comparing quotes - a bare number means nothing without knowing what's inside it.
