The DTC Email Sequence That Generates 30% of Revenue

Most brands send a welcome email and forget about it. This 5-part sequence does the heavy lifting so you don't have to.

Home › Blog › The DTC Email Sequence That Generates 30% of Our Clients' Revenue

Email is still the highest-ROI channel in e-commerce - $36 back for every $1 spent. But most brands are leaving the majority of that value on the table with a single welcome email and a weekly newsletter.

Here is the 5-part automated sequence we implement for every client that consistently drives 25-35% of their total revenue on autopilot.

Email 1: The Welcome (Send Immediately)

Lead with a specific promise - what this customer will get from being on your list. Deliver on any discount or lead magnet you offered. Keep it short, warm, and brand-consistent.

Email 2: The Origin Story (Day 2)

People buy from people. This email is your founder story, your why, your mission. Written in first person. No corporate speak. This is where you turn a subscriber into a fan.

Email 3: The Proof (Day 4)

Social proof in motion. Real customer stories, before/after results, user-generated content. This email does the persuasion work so your product pages don't have to carry the full load.

Email 4: The Education (Day 7)

Teach your subscriber something genuinely useful related to your product category. This positions you as an authority and builds trust without a hard sell.

Email 5: The Offer (Day 10)

Now you have earned the right to sell. A time-limited offer, a bundle deal, or a product recommendation. By email five, subscribers who are going to buy have been warmed up properly - conversion rates are 3-4x higher than a cold campaign.

Email is one lever - pair it with fixing the leaks in your funnel. See 6 reasons stores get traffic but no sales.
