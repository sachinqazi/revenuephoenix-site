How Much Does a Shopify Store Cost in India?

Quotes range from a ₹5,000 template install to a ₹5,00,000+ custom build. Here's an honest breakdown in rupees.

Home › Blog › How Much Does a Shopify Store Cost in India?

If you've priced out Shopify store setup cost in India, you've probably seen everything from a ₹5,000 template install to a ₹5,00,000+ custom build - with no clear sense of which one is right for you. Here's an honest breakdown, in rupees.

DIY with a Theme (₹0–₹15,000)

You pay for your Shopify subscription and maybe a paid theme. This works for a simple catalog with a handful of products. The trade-off is your own time, and a store that rarely converts as well as a custom build.

Freelancer (₹20,000–₹1,50,000)

Rates vary widely by experience. A freelancer is a good fit for a focused build or specific fixes - the risk is what happens after launch, when they move on to the next client.

Agency (₹42,000–₹10,00,000+)

At Revenue Phoenix, Shopify development starts at ₹67,000, with brand strategy starting at ₹42,000 and combo packages from ₹67,000–₹1,00,000 for stores that need both a build and positioning. You're not paying for hours - you're paying for a team that owns the outcome, not just the task.

What Actually Drives the Price Up

Custom design (versus a stock theme), the number of product variants and collections, app integrations (payment gateways, inventory, WhatsApp commerce), and whether you need copywriting and photography alongside the build.

Don't ask "what does a Shopify store cost." Ask "what does a Shopify store cost that actually converts for my specific catalog." See our full global cost breakdown for how this compares internationally, or talk to us directly about your catalog.
