What a Dubai Real Estate Website Actually Needs

We're currently building the website for a Dubai-based real estate consultancy. Here's what this market actually needs to convert.

Home › Blog › What a Dubai Real Estate Website Actually Needs

We're currently building the website for Somara Realty, a Dubai-based real estate consultancy - and it's taught us exactly what a real estate site in this market needs to actually generate leads, not just look good.

Listings UX Is the Whole Game

Off-plan and investment buyers filter aggressively - by budget, handover date, developer, and area. If your listings page can't be filtered fast, buyers bounce to a portal instead of your site.

Lead Capture Has to Be Frictionless

A single generic "contact us" form loses buyers. Property-specific click-to-enquire popups - tied to the exact listing someone was viewing - convert dramatically better than a form at the bottom of the page.

WhatsApp Is Not Optional

In this market, buyers expect to message on WhatsApp, not fill out a form and wait for an email. Every listing and every page needs a direct WhatsApp path.

Social Proof Needs to Be Visual

An embedded, auto-updating Instagram feed of recent listings does more for trust than a paragraph of "About Us" copy.

Founder Story Still Matters

Off-plan investment is a trust purchase. A clear "meet the team" section - not a stock photo - matters more here than in most other real estate markets.
