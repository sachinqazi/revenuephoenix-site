Usually within a few hours during the day. You're messaging the founder directly, not a queue.

Of course. A lot of people message us just to ask questions and plan for later. There's no pressure to commit on the first chat.

Most website builds take a few weeks depending on scope. Social media handling starts almost immediately once we're aligned on strategy. We'll give you a real timeline once we know what you need - not a vague "it depends."

Yes - we currently work with clients across India, the USA, Canada, the UAE, and Australia. WhatsApp makes the time-zone gap a non-issue.
