The Right-Sized Shopify Agency for Small Canadian Brands

Most 'top Shopify agency' lists for Canada are built for brands already doing seven figures. Here's what actually fits a small or early-stage brand.

Home › Blog › A Right-Sized Shopify Agency for Small Canadian Brands

Most "top Shopify agency" lists for Canada are built for brands doing seven figures already. If you're a small or early-stage Canadian brand, that's the wrong size hire - here's what actually fits.

You Don't Need a 50-Person Agency

Large agencies are built for complexity - ERP integrations, multi-entity catalogs, Shopify Plus. If your store is a focused catalog that needs to look sharp and convert, that overhead shows up in your invoice without showing up in your results.

What a Right-Sized Partner Looks Like

Direct access to whoever is actually building your store - no account manager relaying messages. A senior-led, lean team moves faster and costs less than a large agency's junior bench.

What to Ask Before You Hire

"Who will actually build my store?" If the answer is vague, you're buying a brand name, not a builder.

"What happens after launch?" Post-launch support matters more than the build itself for a small brand still finding its footing.

Working Across the Time Zone

We work asynchronously with Canadian clients - clear written updates, fast turnaround, no time-zone bottleneck on decisions.
