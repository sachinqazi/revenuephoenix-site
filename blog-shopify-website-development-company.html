What a Shopify Website Development Company Actually Does

Home › Blog › What a Shopify Website Development Company Actually Does

"Shopify website development company" and "shopify development" get typed into Google a lot, but the phrase hides a wide range of what actually gets delivered. Here's what it should mean - and what to expect when you hire one.

Custom Theme Development vs. Dawn Theme Customization

Two different approaches, both legitimate. Custom Shopify theme development means building a storefront from a blank Liquid codebase - full control, longer timeline. Dawn theme customization means starting from Shopify's own default theme and rebuilding sections, styling, and functionality on top of it - faster, often just as capable for most catalogs. We've done both: custom Liquid for Furrishta and a Dawn theme rebuild for Swati Pearls & Jewellers.

What "Ecommerce Website Development" Should Include

Beyond the storefront itself: checkout optimization, app integrations (payments, inventory, reviews), mobile-first design, and page speed - not just a homepage that looks good in a screenshot.

What a Real Development Company Asks Before Building

"What's your catalog size and structure?" A 10-product store and a 500-SKU store need fundamentally different theme architecture.

"What do you need integrated?" Payment gateways, inventory tools, and marketing apps should be scoped before development starts, not bolted on after.
